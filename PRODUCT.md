# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

CopyKat is for keyboard-heavy macOS users, especially developers, who want to
retrieve and paste clipboard history without interrupting their flow.

## Product Purpose

The website explains and distributes CopyKat, a native macOS menu bar clipboard
manager. Success means a visitor understands how CopyKat works, trusts its local
privacy model, and can download the latest release or get support.

## Positioning

CopyKat combines a Spotlight-style, keyboard-first panel with text, image, file,
pin, and Universal Clipboard support. Clipboard history is stored locally on the
Mac; concealed password-manager entries are ignored and users can exclude apps.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate CopyKat on the marketing site and download releases from
GitHub. In the app, users open the non-activating panel with a configurable
shortcut, search their history, and paste into the active app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Native macOS 14+ app built with SwiftUI and SwiftData.
- Captures text, images, files, and Universal Clipboard items.
- Direct paste uses optional Accessibility permission and falls back to copying.
- History persists locally and its retention is configurable.
- The public website is a static Next.js export hosted on GitHub Pages.
- Website content supports the same 19 locales as the app.

## Brand Commitments

Use the CopyKat cat icon, Kat Orange, cold paper white, graphite black, the
existing display typography, and direct, compact language. The product is free,
MIT licensed, native, and open source.

## Evidence on Hand

- App repository and README: https://github.com/mixxamm/CopyKat
- Website source: https://github.com/mixxamm/copykat-site
- CopyKat and mixxamm logo assets in the existing project and app repository.
- Existing interactive product demonstration and translated marketing copy.

## Product Principles

- Keep the keyboard workflow obvious.
- Make privacy claims specific and verifiable.
- Prefer native product truth over generic clipboard-manager language.
- Keep support direct and human.
- Preserve a fast, static, dependency-light website.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Pages must remain keyboard navigable, responsive, readable in left-to-right and
right-to-left locales, and usable with reduced motion.
